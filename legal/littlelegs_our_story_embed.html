Our Story

In early January 2026, we welcomed a little hedgehog into our family — or "hoglet," if you want to be technically accurate. Like any new family member, we wanted to create a cozy, comfortable, and functional home for our little guy. One essential item was an exercise wheel to help burn off some of that endless nighttime energy.

We bought several wheels from pet stores and online retailers, but none of them were quite right. They were either too large for our enclosure or too small for our little hedgehog.

As a 3D printing enthusiast, I figured someone must have already designed a printable wheel. After a bit of searching, I found one I liked, scaled it to the right size, and printed it. It worked... almost. The running surface simply wasn't wide enough for our hedgehog to run comfortably.

Since I'm also a product designer, I couldn't resist making a few improvements. I widened the running surface, redesigned the base, and gave our little runner the extra space he deserved.

The new wheel worked well, but before long I found myself thinking, I can make this even better. More importantly, I wasn't happy with the all-plastic rotation mechanism. I wanted something smoother, quieter, and built to last. That was the beginning of redesigning the wheel from the ground up.

What I thought would be a simple weekend project quickly grew into something much bigger. After many prototypes (and more than a few lessons learned), I created a stable, durable, whisper-quiet wheel with a unique bearing system. That same design is now at the heart of every LittleLegs wheel we make.

It was Day One of the LittleLegs story. We looked at the finished wheel, smiled, and agreed — it was good.

But then another thought crossed my mind...

Wouldn't it be fun if the wheel had its own little speedometer?

Imagine seeing your pet's speed, distance, and running time displayed right on the wheel, just like the dashboard in a car. So we set out to build exactly that. It meant redesigning the wheel yet again and diving into electronics, but the result was worth every late night.

It was Day Two, and once again, we looked at what we'd built. We were so happy and saw that it was good!

Then came another idea.

Wouldn't it be even better if you could see all those statistics on your phone instead of peeking into the cage and interrupting your tiny athlete's workout?

So we built a mobile app that displays live running statistics on your phone or tablet. Don't worry — we kept the display on the wheel too. We liked it far too much to let it go.

That was Day Three, and when we looked at the app, we were so happy with the result and saw that it was good!

Of course, once you have live statistics, you start wondering...

What about yesterday's run? Last week's? Last month's?

So we added history, trends, average speed, top speed, distance, and running time. Suddenly, you could watch your little runner's progress over time.

That was Day Four. To be fair, Day Four was an exceptionally long day, but when we looked at the result, we were so happy when we saw that it was good!

By now, we realized all that data deserved a permanent home, so we ventured into the cloud. While we were there, we thought we might as well make things more fun. We added colorful charts, graphs, achievements, and other ways to explore your pet's activity and celebrate their accomplishments.

It was "Day 5", and when we looked at the app again, you guessed it — we were so happy — it was good!

Still, we weren't finished.

What's the fun in keeping all those adorable achievements to yourself?

So we made it possible to share your wheel and your pet's progress with friends and family. We also sprinkled the app with interesting facts, helpful tips, and the occasional groan-worthy joke, because every great app deserves at least a few smiles.

By Day Six, the LittleLegs ecosystem had grown well beyond a wheel. You can create running shifts for different pets, manage multiple wheels, organize your furry athletes into running squads, switch between metric and imperial units, receive notifications when a running session begins, and get a summary when it's over.

When we stepped back and looked at everything we've created so far, we were so happy and proud and — with complete bias — thought it was very good. Or, at least good enough to share with you. We hope that this is not where our story ends, as we hope it now continues with you.

We would like to know what you would like to see in the app — how can we make it more useful and more fun for you? What would you like to see? We built in support and feedback mechanisms for just this purpose so that you can share your ideas with us. Whether it's a new feature you'd love to see, a clever idea you've been thinking about, or something that would make caring for your little runner even more enjoyable, we'd love to hear it.
