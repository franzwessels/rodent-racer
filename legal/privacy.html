Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

LittleLegs ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the LittleLegs mobile app and connected wheel device. This policy applies to users worldwide, including those in the United States, the European Union, and the United Kingdom.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Pet activity data — running sessions recorded by your wheel, including distance, speed, duration, and timestamps.
  • Pet details — species, name, and birthday if you choose to provide them. These are optional and used only to personalise your experience.
  • Device information — your wheel's device ID, firmware version, and local network IP address for connection purposes.
  • Notification tokens — device tokens used to send you push notifications about your pet's activity.

2. How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the LittleLegs service:

  • To display your pet's activity history and live stats in the app
  • To send you shift start and session summary notifications
  • To allow you to share your wheel with family members or viewers
  • To personalise cheers, fun facts, and in-app messages based on your pet's species
  • To sync data across your devices via Firebase cloud storage

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the LittleLegs service.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Union and United Kingdom, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide the LittleLegs service you have signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — to maintain security, prevent fraud, and improve the service
  • Consent — for optional features such as push notifications, which you may withdraw at any time

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), hosted in the United States. Firebase is compliant with GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 standards. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections at all times.

Your wheel communicates with our cloud service over HTTPS using a unique per-device secret key. We do not store your home WiFi credentials — these are saved only on the wheel device itself.

For EU/UK users: by using LittleLegs, your data is transferred to the United States. This transfer is covered by Google's Data Processing Terms and Standard Contractual Clauses under GDPR Article 46.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Google Firebase — our cloud infrastructure provider for data storage and authentication
  • Viewers you invite — family members or friends you explicitly invite can view your wheel's activity data. You can revoke access at any time from the app's Settings screen
  • Legal requirements — if required by applicable law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

6. Children's privacy

LittleLegs is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users between 13 and 17 should have parental consent before using the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at support@littlelegs.app and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — request an export of your activity data
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent for push notifications at any time through your device settings

EU/UK users also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@littlelegs.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or remove your wheel, associated session data and personal information is deleted from our servers within 30 days.

9. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, cloud database, and push notifications (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — account authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Sign in with Apple — account authentication on iOS (Apple Privacy Policy)

10. California residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@littlelegs.app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
